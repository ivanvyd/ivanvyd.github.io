Hold. Charge. Bonk!

Hold Dash, let go to bonk

Drag to roll. Hold Dash to charge, let go and BONK! Your rival goes tumbling, and no amount of steering stops the slide. Knock the 11 computer-controlled rivals into the holes before they knock you in.

Survival

Last one rolling wins

The ground flashes a warning, then drops away. Holes open in waves and grow wider while the shore crumbles in. Every round pays Stars, plus a bonus for the win.

Dash

Hold… BONK!

A tap gives a quick nudge. Hold for 0.9 seconds and your hit sends a rival more than three times as far. Rivals charge with the same glowing ring, so watch for it.

Bonk!
★ ★ ●

Boom! Grab skills

Boom · Stone · Float

Skills

Grab it, keep it, fire it

Roll into a skill bubble. It waits on the skill button until you fire it. Caught in a tumble? Fire Stone or Float for a last-second save.

Boom

Wind up for half a second, then blast nearby hamsters away from you.

Stone

Turn to rock for 4 seconds. Nobody can push you, and a rival who dashes into you bounces back.

Float

A balloon lifts you for 3 seconds. Sail over holes and through other hamsters, then drift down onto solid ground.

4 wild islands

Candy Garden · Volcano Isle · Frost Rink · Cosmos

Candy Garden is where it all starts: holes, bumpers, rivals and you. Then Volcano Isle, Frost Rink and Cosmos join in, each with a trick of its own.

★ ★ ●

Free Roam and Practice

Free Roam

Grab every star

Fell in? Roll right back. Star charms sprout across the island, and each one counts the moment you touch it. Grab them fast and the chime climbs higher. Skill bubbles keep popping up and rivals keep bonking for as long as you like.

Practice

Warm up

A calmer round on Candy Garden against 3 rivals who aim slower and hit softer. Learn the dash here, then go big.

★ ★ ★

Open a gift!

The reel rolls, a new look lands

  • The reel rolls past the looks and stops on your new one.
  • A gift never repeats a look you already own.
  • The gift screen shows the odds before you open it:
    • Common 60%
    • Rare 25%
    • Epic 12%
    • Legendary 3%
    Once you own every look of a rarity, its share goes to the others.
  • After 7 gifts in a row with no Epic or Legendary, the next one is Epic or better.
★ ★

Good to know

  • Every rival is a computer-controlled hamster. No account needed.
  • Ads are child-safe for every player: Google AdMob ads rated G and never personalised. Ads never interrupt a round. You may see one between rounds, and the Stars ad plays only when you tap it.
  • Watch an optional ad for 10 Stars. A one-time Remove ads purchase turns off the ads between rounds; the optional Stars ad stays.
  • The Prism ball and the Rainbow trail are sold through Google Play. Gifts never contain them, and Stars can't be bought.
  • Play upright or sideways, on phones and tablets.
  • Separate switches for music, sound and vibration, a Calm motion setting, and the Dash button on either side.
  • 13 languages: English, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish and Ukrainian.
