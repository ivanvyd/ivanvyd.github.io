How it plays

Survival

Stay on the island

The ground cracks, flashes a warning, then drops away. Bounce off the bumpers, stay off the edge and be the last hamster rolling.

Dash

Hold for a big bonk

Hold the Dash button to charge. The longer you hold, the harder and farther your bonk sends a rival tumbling. A quick tap is a quick nudge.

Skills

Boom, Stone, Float

Roll into a skill bubble and fire it with the skill button when the moment is right. Boom pushes everyone away, Stone makes you impossible to push, and Float carries you over the holes.

Free Roam

Grab every star

Fell in? Roll straight back. Every star you touch counts right away, so there is nothing to carry and nothing to drop.

Looks

Find 60 looks

Win Stars every round and open gifts to find new hamsters, balls and trails. The odds are shown in the game, and you never get the same look twice.

Good to know

  • Your rivals are computer-controlled hamsters, and there is no account to make.
  • Ads are child-safe: Google AdMob ads rated G and never personalised. A one-time Remove ads purchase turns off the ads between rounds.
  • Looks are for show, so every hamster plays by the same rules.
  • Plays upright or sideways, on phones and tablets.
  • 13 languages: English, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish and Ukrainian.
