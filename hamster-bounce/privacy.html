Hamster Bounce privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Hamster Bounce is provided by ivanvyd games, the game studio of Vydrin Ivan Denysovych, an individual entrepreneur (FOP) registered in Ukraine. Contact us about this policy at ivan.vydrin.99@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Hamster Bounce does not ask for an account, name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Your progress, Stars, looks and settings are stored on your device. The game also keeps a short play log on the device (rounds played, results and settings, with no names or identifiers). It leaves the device only if you copy it and send it yourself. We do not keep a server copy of any of this.

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android can save the game's progress (Stars, looks and settings) in your device's Google backup, so it comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup belongs to your Google account; we cannot read it. The play log is never included. Without a backup, uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your progress.

Children

The game does not ask for your age. Because it can be played by children, every player is treated as a child for ads. The game has no chat and no social features. Buying something goes through Google Play, which handles any approval it requires, including Family Link.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. They can be optional ads you choose to watch for Stars, or short ads between rounds. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, is limited to content rated G, and is not personalised. Because of that setting, the game shows no consent message. The game does not request the Android advertising ID permission. The Remove ads purchase turns off the ads between rounds.

When the app initializes Google's Mobile Ads SDK or requests an ad, Google may collect, according to Google's disclosure:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location;
  • interactions with the app, such as launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • app set IDs or other identifiers used by the SDK (not the Android advertising ID).

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and sends it encrypted. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and explains its use of data from partner apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Deleting your data

Hamster Bounce has no accounts and no server, so there is nothing for you to sign in to and delete, and we hold no copy of your progress. You can remove everything the game keeps yourself:

  • Progress, Stars, looks, settings and the play log on your device: uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Hamster Bounce, then Storage, and tap Clear storage. This deletes them at once.
  • The Android backup of your progress: it is stored in your Google account, not with us. You can delete it in Google Drive under Storage, then Backups, or turn off backup in your phone's Google settings.
  • Purchases: Google Play keeps the record of what you bought, so that Remove ads and paid looks come back when you reinstall. Google handles that record under its own policies.
  • Data collected by Google for ads: Google AdMob processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and keeps it as described in how Google retains data. You can review and delete Google ad activity at myadcenter.google.com and reset ad settings in your phone's Settings under Privacy, then Ads.

If you ever emailed us, for example to send a play log or ask a question, you can ask us to delete that email and anything in it. Write to ivan.vydrin.99@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data – Hamster Bounce". We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. We do not need any other details from you.

Purchases

You can buy "Remove ads" and some looks through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never receive your payment details. The game checks your purchases with Google Play on the device to unlock what you bought. Stars are earned by playing or by watching an optional ad; they are never sold, and they only open in-game gifts.

App distribution

If you install the game through Google Play, Google handles store and download activity under its own policies. This policy describes the Hamster Bounce app itself.

Changes and contact

If our data practices change, for example when online play is added, we will update this policy before changing the app. Contact ivan.vydrin.99@gmail.com with privacy questions.
