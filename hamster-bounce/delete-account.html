Delete your Hamster Bounce account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for Hamster Bounce, the game by ivanvyd (ivanvyd games, the game studio of Vydrin Ivan Denysovych).

Last updated: 22 October, 2026

You only have an account if you played online. Offline play has no account; its data is only on your device. A parent or guardian can delete a child's account in the same ways.

Option 1: delete in the game (fastest)

To delete your online account and everything the game keeps on this device:

  1. Open Hamster Bounce.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

To delete only your online account and keep your offline progress on this device:

  1. Open Hamster Bounce and tap Play online.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

Our server deletes the account straight away, and the game tells you when it is done. Uninstalling the game does not delete your online account.

Option 2: ask us by email

Use this if you can't open the game, no longer have the device, or are a parent acting for a child.

  1. Write to ivan.vydrin.99@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Delete my Hamster Bounce account.
  3. Include one of these so we can find the account:
    • your friend code (shown as "Friend code" in the game's Online screens, for example ABCD-2345), or
    • your recovery code, if you created one in the Account screen.
  4. If you are a parent or guardian, say that you are asking for your child.

Friends also know your friend code, so if you send only a friend code we may reply with a question to check the account is yours before we delete it. A recovery code proves the account is yours; it stops working once the account is deleted. Never send a recovery code to anyone except us. We don't need your name, address, card details or any other information.

We handle email requests within 30 days and reply to confirm when the account is deleted.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Online account: account ID, sign-in key hash, recovery code hash, accepted termsDeleted at once. The account can no longer sign in.
Generated name and colour, looks, emotes, Stars, trophies, leaderboard rowsDeleted at once. Your card disappears from leaderboards.
Friend code, friends, requests, blocks, parties, recent playersDeleted at once.
Purchase tokens and items bought onlineDeleted at once. Online-only items stop being available to the account.
Reports and moderation records about youDeleted at once.
Reports you made about other playersKept with only a hashed reference to you, until 90 days after the last report about that player.
Room rosters (who was in which match)Kept for 7 days after each match you joined, then deleted.
Your name in other players' recent-player listsNo longer shown, and removed from their lists within about a day.
Purchase records for refunds and fraud checksFor each purchase we keep a scrambled (hashed) purchase reference, the item, whether it was refunded, and the hashed account reference that Google Play also holds. These records contain no name or contact details. They stop the same purchase being claimed by another account and let us match refunds. We keep them while the account exists and delete them 180 days after the account is deleted. A refund notice from Google for a purchase we never recorded holds only a hashed purchase reference and is deleted 180 days after it arrives.
Deletion recordA hashed account reference, the deletion time, and a mark that the old sign-in key and recovery code are cancelled. They hold no name, progress or contact details, and stop the account from coming back. Deleted 180 days after the account.
Encrypted backupsBackups are encrypted and replaced on a rolling schedule, so anything deleted from the server disappears from every backup within about 12 weeks. If we ever restore a backup, we delete again any account that had been deleted.
Your email to us (for email requests)Kept only as long as we need it to handle the request and show that we did.

Data that we don't hold

  • On your device: "Delete my data" in Settings wipes it. You can also uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Hamster Bounce, then Storage, and tap Clear storage.
  • Android backup: if backup is on, your offline progress may be saved in your Google account's backup. Delete it in Google Drive under Storage, then Backups.
  • Google Play purchases: Google Play keeps its own record of what you bought, so items like Remove ads come back when you reinstall. Google handles that record under its own policies.
  • Google ads data: see myadcenter.google.com and the Google Privacy Policy.
