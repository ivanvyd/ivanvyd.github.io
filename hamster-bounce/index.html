Hold. Charge. Bonk!

Hold Dash, let go to bonk

Drag to roll. Hold Dash to charge, let go and BONK! Your rival goes tumbling, and no amount of steering stops the slide. Knock 11 rivals into the holes before they knock you in.

Holes

Watch the ground

The ground flashes a warning, then drops away. In a Battle, holes open in waves and grow wider while the shore crumbles in, so keep rolling toward the middle.

Dash

Hold… BONK!

A tap gives a quick nudge. Hold for 0.9 seconds and your hit sends a rival more than three times as far. Rivals charge with the same glowing ring, so watch for it.

★ ★ ●

Boom! Grab skills

6 skills · new tricks

Skills

Grab it, keep it, fire it

Roll into a skill bubble. It waits on the skill button until you fire it. If you're caught in a tumble, fire Stone or Float for a last-second save.

Boom

Wind up for half a second, then blast nearby hamsters away from you.

Stone

Turn to rock for 4 seconds. Nobody can push you, and a rival who dashes into you bounces back.

Float

A balloon lifts you for 3 seconds. Sail over holes and through other hamsters, then drift down onto solid ground.

Bubble

A bubble wraps around you for 3 seconds and blocks one hit. It even saves you from a comet.

Swap

Trade places with the nearest rival. Great for getting out of a tight spot, or putting someone else in one.

Magnet

For 2 seconds, rivals nearby get pulled toward you. Reel them in, then bonk!

★ ★ ●

7 wild islands

A new trick on every island

Every hazard warns you first, so read the ground and keep rolling. Before a round, a reel rolls through the islands to pick yours.

Candy Garden

Holes and bumpers

Where it all starts. Bounce off the bumpers and steer clear of the holes.

Volcano Isle

Steam vents

Lava surrounds the island. A warning ring fills around a steam vent, then the vent blasts nearby hamsters away.

Cosmos

Comets

Stripes and arrows mark the comet's lane 2.5 seconds ahead. Its striped middle knocks you out like a hole, unless a Bubble saves you, and the rest of the lane flicks you sideways.

Frost Rink

Slippery ice

There's an ice rink in the middle. You're slow to speed up and slow to stop, so it's easy to slide into trouble.

Jungle

Mud and logs

Muddy puddles slow you down, and rolling logs sweep across the island and shove you aside.

Lagoon

Pools and tides

In the shallow pools you're slow to get going and slow to stop. Then a tide surge rolls across and pushes everyone in its path aside.

Clockwork

Oil and pistons

Oil slicks are as slippery as ice, and after a warning, pistons punch nearby hamsters away.

★ ★ ●

Five ways to roll

Five modes and a warm-up

Every mode plays online with players around the world. New here? Start with Practice.

Battle

Last one rolling wins

12 hamsters, one crumbling island. Bonk everyone else into the holes and be the last one rolling.

Team Battle

Last team standing

2 teams of 6 or 3 teams of 4. Teammates bump but never hurt each other, and the last team on the island wins.

Race

Three laps

Follow the flags around the island for 3 laps. Fell off? You roll right back in, so keep going.

Flag Capture

Bring it home

One flag waits in the middle. Grab it and carry it to your team's base. Bonk the carrier and the flag drops!

Free Roam

Grab every star

Star charms sprout across the island, and each one counts the moment you touch it. If you fall in, you roll right back. Skill bubbles keep popping up for as long as you like.

Practice

Warm up

A calmer round on Candy Garden against 3 rivals who aim slower and hit softer. Learn the dash here, then go big.

★ ★ ●

Play online!

Parties · Friends · Emotes · Trophies

Take the bonking online and play with other people. Matches can include computer-controlled players.

Party

Roll together

Make a party and share its 6-letter code. Your party plays on the same team in Team Battle and Flag Capture, and rolls together in Free Roam.

Friends

Add by code

Every player has a friend code. Add a friend with it, see when they're online, and join their Free Roam game or invite them to your party.

Emotes

Say it with emotes

There's no chat. Cheer, wave or say GG with 14 preset emotes: 6 come with the game, 4 come in the Party pack, and 4 more come in the Starter pack and the Signature sets.

Your name

No typing needed

The game gives you a name. Roll a new one, or type a nickname we check before anyone sees it.

Trophies

Climb the board

Win trophies in online rounds, climb the leaderboards and see where your friends are. Your profile shows your matches, wins and bonks.

Fair play

Nothing to buy to win

Name colours and the Party emote pack change how you look, never how you play.

92 looks to collect

Hamsters · Balls · Trails

  • 23 hamster styles
  • 29 balls
  • 30 trails

You start with 3 looks. Earn Stars as you play, then open a gift for 60 Stars. Try a little dragon or an aurora coat, roll in a beach ball, or leave fireflies and comet dust behind you.

Gifts hold 67 looks, everything except the starters and the Signature looks. Looks change your style, not your speed.

★ ★ ★

Open a gift!

The reel rolls, a new look lands

  • The reel rolls past the looks and stops on your new one.
  • A gift never repeats a look you already own.
  • The gift screen shows the odds before you open it:
    • Common 60%
    • Rare 25%
    • Epic 12%
    • Legendary 3%
    Once you own every look of a rarity, its share goes to the others.
  • Every 5 matches fill a free gift. You can also pick a look that gifts hold and unlock it with Stars.
★ ★

Good to know

  • Matches can include computer-controlled players.
  • The game makes your account for you, with no email or password.
  • Online play has no chat. Everyone gets a name from the game and can roll a new one or type a nickname we check before anyone sees it. Players talk with 14 preset emotes. Your online status stays hidden unless you turn off Hide my online status in Settings; then only friends see when you're online.
  • Ads are child-safe for every player: Google AdMob ads rated G and never personalised. Ads never interrupt a round. You may see a short one between rounds, and the Stars ad plays only when you tap it.
  • Watch an optional ad for 10 Stars. Remove ads turns off the ads between rounds; the optional Stars ad stays.
  • Sold through Google Play, each bought once and kept for good: Remove ads, the Starter pack, the Signature looks, hit effects and victory dances, and the Lava King, Space Cadet and Ice Ninja sets. Name colours and emotes need an online account.
  • Paid items are only sold for money. Gifts, Stars and trophies never give them, and Stars can't be bought. Nothing you buy helps you win.
  • Play upright or sideways, on phones and tablets.
  • Separate switches for music, sound and vibration, a Reduced motion switch, a Fixed joystick option, three button sizes, and Mirror controls to put the Dash button on the left.
  • 13 languages: English, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish and Ukrainian.
