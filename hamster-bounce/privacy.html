Hamster Bounce privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Hamster Bounce is provided by ivanvyd. Contact us about this policy at ivan.vydrin.99@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Hamster Bounce does not ask for an account, name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Your progress, Stars, looks and settings, and the age group from the first-launch question, are stored on your device. The game also keeps a short play log on the device (rounds played, results and settings, with no names or identifiers). It leaves the device only if you copy it and send it yourself. We do not keep a server copy of any of this.

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android can save the game's progress (Stars, looks, settings and the age group) in your device's Google backup, so it comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup belongs to your Google account; we cannot read it. The play log is never included. Without a backup, uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes your progress.

The age question

The first time you open the game, it asks for a year of birth. It keeps only the resulting age group, on the device, and uses it to decide whether ads may be shown. We do not receive your answer.

Players under 13

For players under 13 the game never requests or shows ads. A grown-up must pass a check before anything can be bought. The game has no chat and no social features.

Ads for players 13 and older

Players 13 and older may see ads from Google AdMob: optional ads you choose to watch for Stars, and short ads between rounds. Where the law requires it, the game first shows a privacy message so you can make your choices, and you can review them later under Settings → Ad options. Ads for players aged 13 to 15 are requested without personalisation.

When ads are requested, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects, according to Google's disclosure:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location;
  • how you interact with the app, such as launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and sends it encrypted. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and explains how it uses data from partner apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Purchases

You can buy "Remove ads" and some looks through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never receive your payment details. The game checks your purchases with Google Play on the device to unlock what you bought.

App distribution

If you install the game through Google Play, Google handles store and download activity under its own policies. This policy describes the Hamster Bounce app itself.

Changes and contact

If our data practices change, for example when online play is added, we will update this policy before changing the app. Contact ivan.vydrin.99@gmail.com with privacy questions.
