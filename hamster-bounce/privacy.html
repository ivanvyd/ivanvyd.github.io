Hamster Bounce privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October, 2026 · Version 2.2

The short version

  • Matches can include computer-controlled hamsters, and the game does not say which.
  • Online play needs an account. The game makes it from your device. We never ask for your email, a password, your age or your real name.
  • There is no chat. The server gives you a made-up name. Online, you can type a nickname instead if you want. We check it before anyone else can see it, and you can remove it at any time.
  • You can link Google Play Games to move your account to a new device. This is optional, and we do not get your email address.
  • Your friends can see whether you are online or in a match. You can hide this in Settings.
  • Every ad is treated as being shown to a child and is never personalised.
  • You can delete your data from the game at any time, or by email.

If anything here is confusing, ask a parent or another grown-up you trust to read it with you.

2. What stays on your device

The game keeps these things only on your phone or tablet. We do not get a copy:

  • Your game on this device: progress, Stars, looks, settings and language, and rounds played while the connection was down until they are added to your online account.
  • A short help log of rounds played, results and settings, with no names or identifiers. It leaves the device only if you copy it yourself (Settings, then Help & info, then Copy help info) and send it to someone.
  • If you play online: a secret installation key that the game creates at random. It is kept in Android's protected storage and lets this device sign in to your online account.
  • Players you met: the names, looks and trophies of players from your recent rounds as the match showed them, when you met them, and any friend request or block you made that the game keeps only on this device. It keeps up to 30 players for up to 30 days, and longer for the ones you sent a request to or blocked. This list never leaves the device.

If backup is turned on for your Android device, Android can save your game on this device (progress, Stars, looks and settings) in your Google account's backup, so it comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup belongs to your Google account; we cannot read it. The help log and the online installation key are never included. That means a reinstall does not bring back your online account by itself. To move an online account to a new device, sign in with Google Play Games (if you linked it) or use a recovery code (see section 3).

The game does not ask for your age, real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

3. Online play and what our server keeps

Your online account is made the first time you play with an internet connection. Before that, the home screen shows a note that playing online means you accept the terms and this policy. The game then creates an account for you using its installation key. We call this device sign-in. There is no email and no password. Our server keeps only a hash of the key (a scrambled copy that cannot be turned back into the key).

Moving your account to another device. There are two ways:

  • Google Play Games (optional). In your Profile, under Account, you can link your online account to your Google Play Games player. The game asks Google for a one-time code and sends it to our server. Our server gives that code to Google, and Google tells us your Play Games player ID. We store only a hash of your Play Games player ID, so we can find your account again. We do not get your email address or your contacts. On a new device, sign in with Google Play Games and your account moves there. You can unlink Google Play Games there at any time.
  • Recovery code. If you don't use Google Play Games, you can create a recovery code in your Profile, under Account. It looks like XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX. We keep only a hash of it, so we cannot show it to you again. Keep it private. You never need a recovery code to buy anything.

When your account moves to a new device, the old device is signed out and the game tells it that the account moved.

Your nickname (optional). Online, the server gives you a made-up name. You can type a nickname instead: 2 to 16 letters or numbers (at most 2 numbers), spaces, _ or -, with no emoji. Only one account can have each nickname. Before anyone else can see it, we check it in two steps:

  1. Our own rules look for bad words, real full names, phone numbers, email and web addresses, social media names, ages, and names that look like the game, its staff or a computer player.
  2. Then we send the nickname to OpenAI's moderation service, which checks it for harmful words. We send only the nickname text, never your account ID or anything about your device.

If OpenAI does not answer, the nickname waits for up to 10 minutes and other players keep seeing your made-up name. If there is still no answer, the nickname is refused. You can change your nickname once every 24 hours. You can remove it at any time, and other players then see your made-up name again.

When your account is created, the game sends the collection and trophies saved on your device once (earned Stars, free looks and trophies in each mode), so you keep them online. The server limits how much carries over, for example at most 200 trophies in each mode. Paid items are checked with Google Play instead. After that, if your connection drops, the game keeps working, and each round you play meanwhile is sent to your online account when it connects again. For every round this is its Stars and its game mode. For a ranked round it is also the facts that decide its trophies: your place, your bonks, whether you won, your team's place and score, whether it was a draw, whether you left early, and when it was played. Our server works out the trophies from these facts with the same rules as an online match, and counts the round in your match record and toward your next free gift. It adds up to 1500 Stars and 200 trophies a day this way, and counts at most five such rounds a day toward a free gift. A round that reaches our server about 7 days or more after it was played brings only its Stars.

Online play cannot work without the installation key and the game data below.

4. Who can see what

Other players can see: your name and name colour, your hamster, ball and trail looks, your trophies, the emotes you play, and what your hamster does in a match. Your name is your approved nickname if you have one, or your made-up name if you don't. Leaderboards show the same name, looks and trophies with your rank. Your profile page also shows your trophies in each mode and your match record: ranked matches played, wins and bonks. An approved nickname is public: everyone who plays online can see it on player cards, in match lists and on leaderboards.

Your friends and recent players see you in their lists. People can only add you as a friend with your friend code or from their recent players, and you choose whether to accept. If you are in an open party, your friends (players who added you and whom you added) can see that party: its code, its game mode and the cards of the players in it.

Your online status. Your friends (players who added you and whom you added) can see whether you are online (the game's menu checked in with our server in the last 90 seconds), in a match and its game mode, or offline. When you play Free Roam and there is room, they can join your game. Nobody else sees your status: not recent players, and not players you blocked or who blocked you. You can turn on Hide my online status in Settings; your friends then see you as offline and cannot join you.

Nobody else sees: your account ID, installation key, recovery code, whether you linked Google Play Games, your Play Games player ID, your Stars, the looks you own but don't wear, your free gift progress, purchases, country or IP address. A nickname that is waiting for its check, or was refused, is shown only to you.

There is no chat. Nobody can send messages to other players. The only thing players can type is their own nickname, and we check it before anyone else can see it (section 3). Emotes are a fixed set of pictures and animations. You can block or report any player from a match, your friends list, your recent players or a leaderboard, including for a nickname you think is not OK.

Computer-controlled players. Matches can include computer-controlled hamsters (bots) as well as real players. The game does not mark which hamsters are bots. Bots never become your friends and never appear on leaderboards.

7. Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. You can choose to watch an ad to get 10 Stars. There are also short ads between rounds or matches, and the game limits how often they appear. The Remove ads purchase turns off the ads between rounds and matches.

Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, is limited to content rated G, and is never personalised. Because of these settings, the game shows no consent message. The game does not request the Android advertising ID permission. We do not send Google your account ID, name or game data.

When the app starts Google's Mobile Ads SDK or asks for an ad, Google may collect, according to Google's disclosure:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location;
  • interactions with the app, such as launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information about app and SDK performance;
  • app set IDs or other identifiers used by the SDK (not the Android advertising ID).

Google uses this data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, and sends it encrypted. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and explains its use of data from partner apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

8. Purchases

You can buy Remove ads and the Signature items listed in the terms: a ball, a trail, hit effects, victory dances, three sets, the Starter pack, two name colours and an emotes pack.

Google Play handles every payment, and we never receive your card or bank details. Google tells our server which item was bought, its state and a purchase token, through Play Billing and Google Cloud Pub/Sub. We use these to unlock the item, restore it on another device and handle refunds. When you buy online, the game gives Google Play a scrambled (hashed) reference to your online account, so Google can tell us which account a purchase or refund belongs to. Google cannot turn it back into your account ID.

A Google Play purchase belongs to one online account at a time: the account that last showed it to our server. If another online account shows the same purchase, for example after you sign in to the game on another device, the purchase moves to that account and the old account loses the item. When you tap Restore purchases, the game sends our server the purchase tokens of every purchase Google Play reports for the Google account on that device. You never need a recovery code to buy something.

Stars are earned by playing or by watching an optional ad. They are never sold. They open in-game gifts, and the game shows the odds before you open one. Stars can also unlock a gift look you choose. Every five matches you finish fill a free gift. Gifts, free gifts and Stars never give the items you can buy.

12. Deleting your data

In the game (fastest):

  • Settings, then "Delete my data", then hold "Hold to delete everything" deletes your online account on our server, then wipes the game's data on this device, including the list of players you met. The game then starts fresh, and makes a new account the next time you play with an internet connection.
  • Profile (tap your name at the top of the home screen), then under Account tap Delete, then hold "Hold to delete" deletes only your online account. Progress saved on the device stays there, and the game makes no new account until you tap Create new account.

Our server deletes the account at once. You do not need to wait or reinstall.

By email: if you can't open the game, follow the steps on our account deletion page. We handle email requests within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What deleting removes: everything in section 3 that belongs to your account, including your name, nickname and its check records, the link to Google Play Games, looks, Stars, receipts for rounds added later, trophies, your match record, leaderboard rows, friends, blocks, parties, recent players, your online status, purchase tokens, and reports and moderation records about you. Online items you bought stop being available to that account.

What we keep after deleting, and why:

  • A deletion record with a hashed account reference and the deletion time, and a mark that the old installation key and recovery code are cancelled. These records hold no name, progress or contact details. They stop a deleted account from being signed in to or brought back from a backup, and we delete them 180 days after the account was deleted.
  • Reports you made about other players stay with only a hashed reference to you, and end 90 days after the last report about that player. Your place in room rosters ends 7 days after you joined each match.
  • Purchase records needed for refunds and fraud checks: For each purchase we keep a scrambled (hashed) purchase reference, the item, whether it was refunded, and the hashed account reference that Google Play also holds. These records contain no name or contact details. They stop the same purchase being claimed by another account and let us match refunds. We keep them while the account exists and delete them 180 days after the account is deleted. A refund notice from Google for a purchase we never recorded holds only a hashed purchase reference and is deleted 180 days after it arrives.
  • Encrypted backups: Backups are encrypted and replaced on a rolling schedule, so anything deleted from the server disappears from every backup within about 12 weeks. If we ever restore a backup, we delete again any account that had been deleted.

Things only you or Google can delete:

  • Data on your device: uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Hamster Bounce, then Storage, and tap Clear storage. Uninstalling does not delete your online account; delete that in the game or by email first.
  • The Android backup of your game: it is stored in your Google account, not with us. You can delete it in Google Drive under Storage, then Backups, or turn off backup in your phone's Google settings.
  • Your Google Play Games profile: it belongs to your Google account, not to us. Unlinking or deleting your online account removes our link to it, but not the profile itself. Google handles it under its own policies.
  • Google Play purchase records: Google Play keeps the record of what you bought, so that Remove ads and paid looks come back when you reinstall. Google handles that record under its own policies.
  • Data collected by Google for ads: Google AdMob processes it under the Google Privacy Policy and keeps it as described in how Google retains data. You can review and delete Google ad activity at myadcenter.google.com and reset ad settings in your phone's Settings under Privacy, then Ads.
