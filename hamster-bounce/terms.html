To move your online account to another device, you can link it to Google Play Games in your Profile, under Account, and then sign in with Google Play Games on the new device. 5. Names, conduct, reporting and blocking

There is no chat in Hamster Bounce. The server gives every player a made-up name, and you can swap it for another made-up name. Online, you can also type a nickname. Emotes are a fixed set.

Nickname rules. Your nickname is public, so keep it friendly and safe. Don't use your real name or anyone else's, a phone number, an email or web address, a social media name, your age, rude or hurtful words, or a name that pretends to be the game, its staff or another player. We check every nickname with our own rules and OpenAI's moderation service before other players can see it, and we may refuse it. Only one account can have each nickname. You can change your nickname once every 24 hours and remove it at any time. Other players can report a nickname, and a nickname that breaks these rules counts as breaking these terms (see Bans below).

Be kind in how you play and which emotes you use. Don't follow, gang up on or keep targeting another player.

Friends and online status. Your online status is hidden until you turn off Hide my online status in Settings. Then your friends can see whether you are online, in a match or offline, and can join your Free Roam game while it has room. Turn the setting back on to stop that.

You can block a player, which removes them from your friends and stops them sending you friend requests, joining your party or game, and seeing your online status, and report a player from a match, your friends list, your recent players or a leaderboard. We review reports.

Bans

If an account breaks these terms, a person on our team may ban it from online play for a time or for good, depending on what happened. Banned players keep what is saved on their device and can still delete their data.

Appeals. If you think we banned your account by mistake, email ivan.vydrin.99@gmail.com with the subject "Hamster Bounce appeal" and your friend code. A person will look at it again and reply, normally within 30 days.

6. Computer-controlled players

Matches can include computer-controlled hamsters (bots), for example to fill empty seats so a match can start quickly. The game does not mark which hamsters are bots, so a hamster you play against may be a bot rather than a real person. Bots never become your friends and never appear on leaderboards.

7. Stars and virtual items

Stars are earned by playing or by choosing to watch an ad. Stars are never sold and cannot be bought with money. They open in-game gifts that contain looks. The game shows the odds of each gift before you open it.

Star unlocks. You can also use Stars to unlock one look you choose from the looks that gifts contain. The game shows its Star price, which depends on the look's rarity, before you unlock it.

Free gifts. Every match you finish, except in Free Roam, fills one step of a free gift, and the game shows your progress. After five steps you can open a free gift. It works like a gift opened with Stars, with the same odds, but costs no Stars. Rounds that are added to your account after the connection dropped fill at most five steps a day.

Stars, gifts, looks, emotes, name colours, badges and trophies are part of the game. They change how things look, not how strong you are. They have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money, and cannot be sold or traded, inside or outside the game. You have a licence to use them in the game, as long as these terms allow; you do not own them.
